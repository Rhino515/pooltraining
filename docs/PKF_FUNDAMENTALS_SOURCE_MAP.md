- **Source pack:** `/workspace/pkf-fund-src/PKF_Fundamentals_PDF_009.jpg` … `_034.jpg` (26 original PDF pages).

**Course order:** the 12 sections follow PKF's own headings and book order (pages 2 → 22). The order is Fundamentals (shooting line/ghost ball → stance → forearm & grip → back/forward stroke & follow through → tip position & eyes → putting it together & the stroke drill → stroke memory), then Bridges / Stances (stances → open & closed → tripod → rail bridges → tight spots).

**Repetition counts:** none are enforced. The app only logs practice sessions. PKF's own words are quoted, with the app's note "The app does not set a count", in these lessons:
- `sd-felt` and `sd-practice-felt` ("several hundred shots", printed p11 / PDF 20)
- `sm-memory` and `sm-q-ready` ("hundreds, perhaps thousands", "one or two hundred shots", "thousands of balls", printed p12 / PDF 21)

**Ghost Ball** is taught only as PKF presents it (figures 1-1 to 1-3). No other aiming system appears.

**Cue tip:** only the fundamental distance PKF teaches here: "less than one cube of chalk" (p9) and the tip stopping on/near the felt (p10). Spin/English is not taught. Page 20 mentions draw only as a bridge-angle situation (start level, then lower the tip), which is quoted as PKF wrote it.

**Source-review flags:** none. Every lesson maps to a printed page and figure.

## 2. App mechanics (not PKF claims)

These are app infrastructure, not PKF claims:
- **LOCK ANSWER before reveal.** Identification items use photo-only crops so the printed captions stay hidden. Text-only items show the figure after LOCK, because the page prints the answer.
- **Unlocking:** a section unlocks at 70% on knowledge checks. The exam passes at 80% knowledge.
- **Self-evaluation ratings:** COMPLETED / NEEDS MORE WORK, COMFORTABLE / INCONSISTENT / NEEDS PRACTICE, COMFORTABLE / NEEDS PRACTICE, and COMPLETED / NOT YET for exam checkpoints. They never gate progress and never fail a player.
- **Progress tracking:** the session log, the REVIEW FUNDAMENTALS and NEEDS PRACTICE lists, and Dev Mode preview (nothing saved).

The app never claims to validate mechanics.
